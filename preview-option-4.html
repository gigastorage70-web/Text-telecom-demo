Technical FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions regarding interconnects, SMPP binds, or wholesale pricing? Find direct answers here.

What connection protocols do you support? +
We provide both high-throughput SMPP v3.4 transceiver binds (over secure TLS or standard port) and high-speed RESTful JSON APIs with sub-second webhook delivery confirmations.
How fast can our account be provisioned? +
Standard API and SMPP test binds are provisioned within 1 hour after interconnect verification. Dedicated throughput ports can be assigned on the same business day.
Do you offer custom Sender ID registration? +
Yes, we support dynamic alphanumeric sender IDs across supported destinations, as well as local regulatory pre-registration across UAE, Saudi Arabia, UK, and APAC operators.
What is your SLA and uptime guarantee? +
Text Telecom offers a guaranteed 99.99% network uptime SLA backed by geo-redundant node clusters and 24/7 continuous NOC monitoring.
